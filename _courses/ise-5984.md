<h2>Course description</h2>
(3 Credit Hours) Introduction to computer vision and explainable AI methods for smart systems, with applications in manufacturing and other industrial operations. Key imaging modalities and image processing techniques. Neural networks, convolutional architectures, attention mechanisms, and selected generative models for classification, regression, object detection, segmentation, and image generation. Selected geometric vision methods for pose estimation and augmented reality. Emphasis on fundamental concepts and methods in interpretability and explainability, including feature attribution, perturbation analysis, counterfactual explanations, and explanation evaluation.
